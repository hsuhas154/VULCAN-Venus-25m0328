Source
  S. Perez-Hoyos et al., "Venus upper clouds and the UV absorber from MESSENGER/MASCS
  observations", Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets, vol. 123, pp. 145 to 162, 2018,
  doi 10.1002/2017JE005406. Figure 8, middle panel (journal page 153): reflectivity I/F
  of MASCS spectrum 151 against wavelength. The caption says the error ranges of the
  data are drawn in grey and the best fit model in black.

What was digitised
  The figure is a single embedded greyscale raster in the published PDF (827 x 1373
  pixels, 300 ppi). It was extracted with pdfimages, without resampling.
  Axis calibration, from the tick marks of the middle panel (pixel column or row, value):
x: lambda_um = 0.00169184*col + 0.099619, ticks [(np.float64(177.5), np.float64(0.4)), (np.float64(295.5), np.float64(0.6)), (np.float64(414.5), np.float64(0.8)), (np.float64(532.0), np.float64(1.0)), (np.float64(650.5), np.float64(1.2)), (np.float64(768.5), np.float64(1.4))]
y: IF = -0.00110878*row + 1.347630, ticks [(np.float64(674.5), np.float64(0.6)), (np.float64(764.5), np.float64(0.5)), (np.float64(854.5), np.float64(0.4)), (np.float64(944.5), np.float64(0.3)), (np.float64(1125.5), np.float64(0.1))]
  Each pixel column between 309 and 700 nm was read top to bottom. Every pixel darker
  than 215 of 255 (grey data or black fit) counts as drawn. Columns that hold an axis
  tick mark were skipped, and so were the left-edge tick rows.
    IF_low   value at the lowest drawn pixel in the column
    IF_high  value at the highest drawn pixel in the column
    IF_mid   value at the median drawn pixel in the column
  IF_low to IF_high is therefore the drawn envelope of the observation including its
  plotted error range; it is not a new uncertainty estimate.

Limits
  One pixel is about 1.7 nm in wavelength and 0.0011 in I/F. The panel begins near
  305 nm, so nothing below that wavelength can come from this source. This is one
  spectrum at one geometry, not a disc average.

The data behind the published figures were posted by the authors at a web address
given in the paper; that host no longer resolves (checked 19 October 2026), so the
figure itself is the only source available.
